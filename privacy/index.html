Privacy Policy — InspireCreativity

Last updated: June 8, 2026

InspireCreativity ("the app", "we", "us") is an iOS app that lets you browse and copy hand-crafted SwiftUI animation code. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and your choices. We designed the app to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

Account information (only if you create an account). Creating an account is optional — you can browse the entire catalog and purchase Pro without one. If you do sign up, we collect:

  • Email address — used to authenticate you, send verification and password-reset emails, and recover your account. It is not used for advertising and is never sold.
  • Name (first and last) — used to personalize your account. It is not used for advertising and is never sold.
  • A user identifier — assigned by our authentication provider to associate your account with your sign-in.

Authentication is handled by Supabase (supabase.com), acting as our data processor. Your email and credentials are stored on Supabase infrastructure.

Purchases. The Lifetime unlock (a one-time purchase) is processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. We never receive or store your payment-card details. Apple shares only the transaction/entitlement information needed to unlock Pro on your devices.

On-device data. Favorites and basic preferences are stored locally on your device using UserDefaults. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

How we use your information

We use the limited information above only to: operate your account, verify your email, let you reset your password, deliver your Pro entitlement, and respond to support requests.

Data retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the app. This removes your account and associated authentication data from our systems. You may also email us to request deletion.

Children

InspireCreativity is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
